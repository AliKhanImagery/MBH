# Vendor-Neutral SEO & Branding Rule

**Status:** Active
**Scope:** All copywriting, UI components, and SEO content.

## Context
MBH Solutions is an engineering company and systems integrator with a multi-principal, world-grade proven track record. MBH does **not** sell their equipment as the "only solution."

## Rule
1. **Never use proprietary equipment names from vendors/principals (e.g., "CONTIMAXX") as the primary label or technical anchor.** 
2. Always replace proprietary equipment names with broad, SEO-friendly engineering terminology (e.g., "Continuous Dissolving Systems").
3. This ensures MBH is positioned as an independent engineering integrator and captures generic search engine volume.

## Exceptions
- Control systems and PLC architectures (e.g., Siemens S7-300, S7-1200, S7-1500) are acceptable to mention, as they describe the underlying engineering standard/layer rather than a proprietary OEM hardware machine.
